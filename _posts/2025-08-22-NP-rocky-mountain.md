## 2025.08.22 - 2025.08.25
Immediately felt the power of high altitude when we arrived at the Alpine Visitor Center on day 1, sitting at 11,796 feet, the highest visitor center in the US National Parks. Amazing high peak views, and saw a lot of elks, including one very close at the trail.
