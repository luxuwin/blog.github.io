## 2025.12.21 - 2025.12.22
We visited 2 of the islands: Anacapa and Santa Cruz. These islands can only be accessable by boats, and tickets need to be booked well ahead of time. Hikes on these islands were wonderful, sometimes it feels like the entire island is just you and the sea birds. Wish I could come back one day and camp overnight in one of the islands.
