## 2024.12.28
The Forrest Gump Point on U.S. Route 163 in Utah is crowded even in winter, where Forrest stopped running after 3 years in the famous movie. The monuments here in the Monument Valley Navajo Tribal Park look eternal, but they are actuall the best evidence showing how time can shape and change the landscape.
